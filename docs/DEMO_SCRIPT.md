# Demo video script (under 3:00)

Judges are not required to watch past 3 minutes, so aim for 2:45. Use only footage of the real app working. No copyrighted music: use the app's own sounds, or royalty-free audio you have rights to.

| Time | Picture | Voice-over (draft) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Candlelit telegraph office; the brass key clicks; paper tape prints | "In 1848, two sisters in New York said they could talk to the dead, one knock at a time. The newspapers called it the spiritual telegraph." |
| 0:15–0:30 | Cut to a person in bed, still; only their eyebrows move | "Today, people with ALS, locked-in syndrome or a breathing tube can often still blink or raise an eyebrow, but they can't speak. Speech devices are expensive and need special hardware." |
| 0:30–0:40 | Title card: **Spirit Telegraph** | "Spirit Telegraph brings back the telegraph key so the living can speak. It works with a webcam and a browser." |

| 1:20–1:50 | Caregiver asks aloud: "Are you cold?" → "Heard:" appears → spirit-ink replies fade in → NEXT, ACCEPT → speaks "Yes, a blanket please" | "When someone asks a question, Spirit Telegraph suggests replies. Two movements instead of twenty. And it never says anything you didn't choose." |

(Change the numbers to what you actually shipped.) |
| 2:45–2:55 | Telegram stamps "THANK YOU STOP" | "Spirit Telegraph. Give the living a voice." |

## Filming notes
- Film yourself as the user. If you film anyone else, especially a person with a disability or a patient, get their written consent first.
- Record the screen at 1080p. Add captions, since judges may watch muted.
- Show the deployed URL on screen at least once.
